We’re Hiring! Addictions & Mental Health Counsellor

Tsi Kanonhkhwatsheríyo Indigenous Interprofessional Primary Care Team (IIPCT) is looking for a compassionate and dedicated Addictions & Mental Health Counsellor to join our team!

Location: Shannonville, Ontario
Compensation: $32.78 – $40.76 per hour

Show the full post

This position plays an important role in supporting individuals and families experiencing substance use and mental health challenges through culturally grounded, trauma-informed counselling, harm reduction, advocacy and recovery support.

Working alongside our multidisciplinary team and Traditional Healing services, the successful candidate will help support wholistic wellness and access to both traditional and Western approaches to healing.

We're looking for someone with:

Registration as an RSSW, RSW or RP in Ontario

A minimum of 2 years of relevant addictions counselling experience

Experience working with Indigenous individuals, families and communities

A commitment to providing compassionate, culturally safe and person-centred care

AnnouncementJune 24, 2026IIPCT

A New Story on Our Walls

We are proud to share the beautiful new mural now featured at our Tyendinaga clinic (45 Meadow Drive), created by local Indigenous artist Kory Parkin.

This stunning piece brings together the teachings of the Medicine Wheel and the Four Sacred Medicines, weaving together stories of the seasons, directions, animals, clans, and the natural world. At its centre stands the Great Tree of Peace, rooted on the back of the turtle, reminding us of our connection to Turtle Island, community, healing, and balance.

Show the full post

Every element of this mural carries meaning—from Grandmother Moon and the Sun overhead, to the sacred medicines, clan animals, and Creation Story teachings flowing through the water below. It serves as a powerful reflection of Indigenous knowledge, culture, and the values that guide the care we provide.
