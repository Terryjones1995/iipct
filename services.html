Cultural Services

Traditional healing, spiritual support and cultural learning.

Our Cultural Services blend traditional healing, spiritual support and cultural learning to guide clients on a path of holistic wellness.

Our Traditional Healing Coordinator connects clients with seers and knowledge holders from Rotinonhsyonni (Haudenosaunee) and other First Nations. This makes sure medicines and guidance are used safely, whether alone or alongside pharmaceuticals.

Language, culture and identity are central to this care. Through discussions with seers and mental health professionals, clients regain direction and connection. Traditional knowledge is used as a preventative and supportive approach for overall wellness.

To connect with our Traditional Healing Coordinator, please contact us.

Mental Health Services

Supporting adults, with linkage for pre- and post-natal mental health care.

The Mental Health Program offers short-term services for individuals aged 18 and older who receive care through Tsi Kanonhkhwatsheríyo.

Using a wholistic approach that blends Western and Traditional healing practices, the Mental Health Worker provides strengths-based and trauma-informed support to promote balance and well-being.

In an emergency, call 911 or go to the nearest emergency department.

Short-term services include

  • Psychosocial assessment
  • Goal setting and Plan of Care development
  • Resource connections and referrals
  • Education and guidance
  • Case management
  • Group workshops and programs
  • A final wrap-up appointment to review goals and next steps

Health Support Services

Care coordination across all systems, with ongoing programs to promote wellness.

Community health programs

Culturally safe programs help increase education and promote wellness for Indigenous people living in the South East LHIN region. Support includes help with forms, funding, and access to essential community resources.

  • Help with forms for IIPCT clients: Ontario Works, Ontario Disability Support Program, Indian Day Schools and Water Claims
  • Funding help through the Assistive Devices Program, NIHB and other available programs
  • Guidance with community services for housing and food security

Smoking cessation support

We provide access to the STOP (Smoking Treatment for Ontario Patients) Program, a province-wide program that delivers smoking cessation treatment and counselling. It is offered through a partnership with the Canadian Mental Health Association.

  • 26 weeks of free Nicotine Replacement Therapy (NRT): patches (21 mg, 14 mg, 7 mg), Nicorette gum (2 mg), Thrive lozenges (2 mg) and the Nicorette inhaler (4 mg)
  • Smoking cessation counselling with goal setting, journaling, and one-on-one or phone-based support (weekly, biweekly or monthly)
  • An Indigenous Quit Kit, available on request

Getting care

How to get care

Our priority population is all people who identify as Indigenous, and their families, in our regions. All our services are status blind.

Already an IIPCT patient?

Registered patients can book a visit online. You can also call your clinic.

Schedule Your Visit

New to IIPCT?

Are you Indigenous and in need of a family doctor or primary care provider, or hoping to find care closer to home? Our Deseronto clinic is accepting patients for its waitlist. To join, call and speak to our Reception. You must be Indigenous (Status or Non-Status) to register with our clinic.
