Full Moon Ceremony – Honouring Grandmother Moon

Tuesday, November 24, 2026 · 6:00 pmShannonville Office, 14 York Road, Unit 4, Shannonville

Free Massage Appointments

Friday, November 27, 2026 · 9:00 am–12:00 pmThe last of the November dates (Fridays, November 6, 13, 20 and 27).Shannonville Office, 14 York Road, Unit 4, Shannonville

Students from the Loyalist College Massage Therapy Program are offering free 45–60 minute massage appointments at our Shannonville location throughout November.

All appointments are completed by students under the supervision of a Registered Massage Therapist (RMT) Faculty Member.

Who can come

You must identify as Indigenous, live within an Indigenous household, or be a rostered patient of an IIPCT clinic.

How to take part

Book online. Spaces are limited. For more information, call (343) 478-0196 ext. 106 or email kendra.garvin@iipct.com.

With the Loyalist College Massage Therapy Program.

Workshop

Will Planning Presentation

Monday, November 30, 2026 · 3:00–4:30 pmShannonville Office, 14 York Road, Unit 4, Shannonville

Planning ahead is an important step in protecting yourself and your loved ones! Learn about the basics of wills and Powers of Attorney, why planning ahead matters, and what costs may be involved. There will also be an opportunity to ask questions.

All attendees will be entered into a draw for a Life File Box filled with prizes. Light refreshments will be provided.

Who can come

IIPCT rostered clients and Indigenous community members, both on and off Territory.

How to take part

Registration is required. For more information, contact Shelley Loft, Indigenous Lifelong Care Coordinator, at (343) 478-0196 ext. 115 or shelley.loft@iipct.com.

Facilitated by Queen’s Elder Law Clinic.

Dec9Wed
Partner event

Social Songs & Dance

Wednesdays, 6:00–8:00 pm: December 9, 2026; January 13, February 10, March 10 and April 14, 2027Tsi Tyónnheht Onkwawén:na Language and Cultural Centre, 39 Salmon River Road, Shannonville (planned location)

Join us to learn social songs and dances! Whether you are joining for the first time or have been dancing for years, everyone is encouraged to take part.

Bring rattles, plates, cups, or feast baskets if you have them. Ribbon skirts and/or ribbon shirts are encouraged. Bring a refillable water bottle. Indoor shoes must be worn. Light snacks and refreshments will be provided.

Who can come

Open to all MBQ members and community. Youth under 16 must be accompanied by an adult.

How to take part

More details for each evening will be shared as they become available. For the October and November evenings, registration was encouraged and drop-ins were welcome. Questions: Tracey Gazley, traceyg@mbq-tmt.org, or Nancy Gourlay, events@kenhteke.org.

With Mohawk Family Services, the Family Wellbeing Program and the Tsi Tyónnheht Onkwawén:na Language and Cultural Centre.

Programs That Repeat

Regular Programs

These programs come back regularly. The next dates are posted on Facebook when they are set.

Breakfast & Beads

A monthly beading circle

Saturday mornings, 9:00 am–12:00 pm, at 14 York Road, Unit 4, Shannonville, with a light breakfast and all supplies provided. In 2026 it ran on April 18, May 2, August 22, October 3 and November 14. You must register for each session, and spots fill quickly.

Full Moon Ceremony

About once a month, near the full moon

An evening ceremony at 14 York Road, Unit 4, Shannonville, for those who identify as women. In 2026: May 1, May 31, July 2, July 29, August 28, September 25, October 26 and November 24.

Free student massage

Friday mornings, in monthly blocks

Students from the Loyalist College Massage Therapy Program, 9:00 am–12:00 pm at 14 York Road, Unit 4, in September, October and November 2026. Booking opens at 8:30 am on a set day and fills fast.

Visiting healers and seers

About every one to two months

A traditional healer or seer visits for appointments in Shannonville, Bancroft and Kingston (in 2026: May, June, July, August and November). Priority goes to IIPCT rostered patients, and some sessions are only for them. Booking is by phone with our team.

Lifelong Care workshops

About one a month

Often the same workshop at three locations a week apart: advance care planning, caregiver support, death doula training, scrapbooking with the Alzheimer Society, and will planning. Ask Shelley Loft, Indigenous Lifelong Care Coordinator, ext. 115.

Community cancer screening days

Spring and fall

In May and October 2026, from 4:30–7:00 pm at our clinics, for people without a family doctor or nurse practitioner. Booked by phone.

Strawberry Social

Every June

Held each June since 2023. The 4th Annual Strawberry Social was on June 25, 2026, at the Deseronto Community Centre, with social songs and dances, an Indigenous makers market, and free strawberries and ice cream.

Annual General Meeting

Once a year

The 5th Annual General Meeting of Tsi Kanonhkhwatsheríyo was held online on September 18, 2026.
