Questions and Answers

Tap a question to see the answer.

  • How do I book an appointment?
    If you are a registered patient, you can book online on our Schedule Your Visit page. You can also call (343) 478-0196 and press the number for your location.
  • Is IIPCT taking new patients?
    The Deseronto Clinic is accepting patients for its waitlist. To join, call (343) 478-0196, press 4, and speak to Reception, Monday to Friday, 8:30 am – 4:30 pm. You must be Indigenous (Status & Non-Status) to register with our clinic.
  • Who can get care at IIPCT?
    We focus on people who identify as Indigenous, and their families, in our regions. All our services are status blind: Status and Non-Status people are welcome.
  • What services does IIPCT offer?
    Four programs: Cultural Services, Primary Medical Care, Mental Health Services and Health Support Services. See all our services.
