Serve Community Through Culturally Grounded Care

At IIPCT, we provide whole-person, culturally safe primary care that honours Indigenous ways of knowing, health and well-being.
Our team works in partnership with individuals, families, and communities to deliver compassionate, inclusive, and community-led care.

Why Work at IIPCT

A Meaningful Career
with Lasting Impact

Be part of a collaborative, values-driven team that centres Indigenous health, community wellness, and whole-person care. At IIPCT, your work contributes to healthier people, stronger communities, and more culturally grounded health care close to home.
